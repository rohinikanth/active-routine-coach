# My agent: ActiveRoutine Coach & Productivity Companion

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps busy individuals maintain consistent workout routines and stay active throughout the workday with a catalog of micro-workouts, habit tracking, and personalized scheduling.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User fitness goals, daily availability, equipment access, fatigue levels, and historical workout/streak logs.
- Tools: Look up exercise routines filtered by time/equipment, log completed workouts and habits, update daily progress.
- Catalog/UI: Workout cards (exercises, sets, reps), daily activity routine table, and habit streak dashboards.
- Image gen: Motivational visual cards or exercise form demonstrations.
- Sandbox: Calculates daily completion percentages, calorie expenditure, and weekly volume progression.

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI workout cards, calorie/volume sandbox calculator, image generation for workout motivational badges
First eval question: "I have 15 minutes between meetings and no equipment, what micro-workout can I do right now to stay on track?"
